Hide Alarm

A house, a garden, a garage and loads of hiding spots. Do you want to hide from the seeker or be the seeker?

Controls: WASD / arrow keys or drag on the playfield, mouse wheel zooms, E hides you or searches a hiding spot, Shift sneaks. Hide: You and three kids against the seeker. Survive 100 seconds. Seek: Find all four kids in 120 seconds. H makes the nearest kid giggle (3× per round, sound on).
